---
name: Review panel wording
description: Transparency convention for the app download page’s sample review panel.
---

The download page’s review-style panel uses generated/sample content, so its visible labels should describe it as example user experiences or community feedback. It must not imply that the entries are verified Google Play reviews or official store data.

**Why:** Presenting generated names, dates, ratings, and comments as verified reviews could mislead visitors.

**How to apply:** Preserve the transparent “contoh pengalaman pengguna” framing whenever the panel copy, modal title, or related call-to-action is edited.
